About ALDEMARINE

ALDEMARINE is a local marine and project platform for Kazakhstan and the Caspian region. The company accepts requests, coordinates projects, performs confirmed survey and documentation services, arranges supplies, works with ports, vessels and contractors, files and manages documents, and brings in engineering partners when needed.

How we work with partners

ALDEMARINE is transparent about the delivery layer behind every task.

Direct

Delivered by ALDEMARINE

ALDEMARINE organises and delivers the work.

  • Marine Supply and marine agency support
  • Cargo documentation, cargo readiness, mobilisation support
  • Tender and qualification support
  • Cargo survey and MWS — only within the confirmed scope of the appointed surveyor
Coordinated

Coordinated by ALDEMARINE

ALDEMARINE manages the process and documents; the technical decision sits with a partner or authority.

  • Preparing and submitting documentation to class, flag, registry
  • Towage documentation (register, data, partner liaison)
Engineering partner

Delivered by qualified partners

The calculation and signature sit with the partner, not ALDEMARINE.

  • Lifting plans, sea-fastening, lashing, RAMS, stability and towage calculations
  • Independent risk review — only by separate written agreement
  • Vessel-condition surveys — partner surveyor, subject to confirmation per project
Authority decision

Authority approvals — not ALDEMARINE

ALDEMARINE does not issue or guarantee these decisions.

  • Approval by a classification society, flag administration or registry
  • Certificate of Approval
  • Insurer acceptance

Scope of responsibility

Marine Warranty Survey & marine survey

ALDEMARINE provides Marine Warranty Survey and marine survey services within the confirmed competence and scope of the appointed surveyor. Each engagement is subject to project requirements, client appointment and applicable insurer, class or project-owner conditions.

Class, flag & registry

ALDEMARINE prepares, coordinates and manages the submission of vessel and marine-operation documentation to the relevant classification society, flag administration or registry. Final approval remains with the competent authority.

Engineering calculations

Delivered through qualified engineering partners; ALDEMARINE coordinates the request and logistics but does not sign the calculation under its own name.

Port coverage

Confirmed coverage on the Caspian.
