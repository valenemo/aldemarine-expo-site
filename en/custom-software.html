Example work · Custom maritime documents and software

Safety Management System manuals, built as a product

We prepare Safety Management System manuals for shipping companies and vessels: a company manual (Level A, the basis for the Document of Compliance) and a ship manual (Level B, the basis for the Safety Management Certificate). Each ship manual is assembled from a library of procedures and checklists to match the vessel.

Company manual

Procedures and checklists for all sixteen elements of the ISM Code: responsibilities, resources, maintenance, incidents, audits, certification, security, records and cyber risk.

Ship manual for the vessel

Modules are switched on or off per vessel profile: offshore supply and anchor handling with DP, bulk carrier, product tanker, chemical tanker, gas carrier, container ship.

Every rule has its source

Each statement points to its source (IMO, class, flag State, industry guidance) or is marked as a company decision. Page references are checked by a program, not by eye.

Linked to maintenance

Equipment templates per vessel class. Intervals follow the maker’s manual. Work for specialised companies, such as radio and fire systems, is booked and closed only with their report.
