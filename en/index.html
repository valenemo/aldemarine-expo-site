What ALDEMARINE delivers — and what it does not

Four layers: direct delivery, coordination, partners and authority decisions. Details for every service.

Direct

Delivered by ALDEMARINE

ALDEMARINE organises and delivers the work.

  • Marine Supply and marine agency support
  • Cargo documentation, cargo readiness, mobilisation support
  • Tender and qualification support
  • Cargo survey and MWS — only within the confirmed scope of the appointed surveyor
Coordinated

Coordinated by ALDEMARINE

ALDEMARINE manages the process and documents; the technical decision sits with a partner or authority.

  • Preparing and submitting documentation to class, flag, registry
  • Towage documentation (register, data, partner liaison)
Engineering partner

Delivered by qualified partners

The calculation and signature sit with the partner, not ALDEMARINE.

  • Lifting plans, sea-fastening, lashing, RAMS, stability and towage calculations
  • Independent risk review — only by separate written agreement
  • Vessel-condition surveys — partner surveyor, subject to confirmation per project
Authority decision

Authority approvals — not ALDEMARINE

ALDEMARINE does not issue or guarantee these decisions.

  • Approval by a classification society, flag administration or registry
  • Certificate of Approval
  • Insurer acceptance
