Marine Surveys & Marine Warranty Survey

Every survey type states what is checked, who signs, and the limitations. Cargo surveys, vessel surveys, class and OVID are deliberately kept separate.

Direct Pre-OVID Readiness Review
Purpose

Internal readiness check ahead of an official OCIMF OVID inspection.

When needed

Ahead of a scheduled vessel OVID inspection.

What is checked

Safety management, equipment, documentation, crew familiarity — against the VIQ checklist.

Report

Internal gap-analysis report (not an official OVID record).

Site/vessel visit

Yes.

Engineering calculation

No — this is an internal review, not an engineering calculation.

Who is responsible

ALDEMARINE.

This is NOT an official OVID inspection and does not substitute an OCIMF-accredited inspector.

Direct · confirmed scope only Cargo Condition Survey
Purpose

Assesses cargo condition, quantity and quality at load, in transit or on discharge.

When needed

For project and general cargo requiring condition confirmation at a point in time.

What is checked

Cargo condition, stowage, packaging, quality sampling, count/tally verification.

Report

Cargo condition/quality survey report.

Site/vessel visit

Yes.

Engineering calculation

No.

Who is responsible

The ALDEMARINE surveyor personally.

Limited to cargo, not the vessel; distinct from draft survey (weight only) and damage survey (investigative).

Direct · confirmed scope only Damage Survey
Purpose

Investigates and quantifies loss or damage after an incident, for claims purposes.

When needed

After a reported cargo incident.

What is checked

Extent and cause of damage, repair scope/cost estimate, evidence preservation.

Report

Damage survey report supporting a claim.

Site/vessel visit

Yes, often at short notice.

Engineering calculation

No.

Who is responsible

The ALDEMARINE surveyor personally, maintaining independence.

This is an investigative survey — not to be blurred with a routine cargo condition survey.

Direct · confirmed scope only Loading / Discharge Survey
Purpose

Monitors a loading or discharge operation: sequence, quantities, stability/stress limits.

When needed

During loading or discharge of project/general cargo.

What is checked

Loading/discharge rate and sequence, draft/trim checks, tally, hold condition.

Report

Loading/discharge survey report (often combined with a draft survey).

Site/vessel visit

Yes, throughout the operation.

Engineering calculation

No.

Who is responsible

The ALDEMARINE surveyor personally.

Distinct from cargo condition survey (quality) and draft survey (quantity), though often run in parallel.

Coordinated Lashing / Securing Inspection
Purpose

Verifies that cargo (general/project/container) is adequately secured for the voyage.

When needed

Before departure with project or oversized cargo.

What is checked

Lashing materials and points, compliance with the Cargo Securing Manual, calculated forces.

Report

Lashing/securing inspection report or compliance certificate.

Site/vessel visit

Yes, on completion of lashing before sailing.

Engineering calculation

Yes — the lashing calculation is performed by an engineering partner.

Who is responsible

ALDEMARINE surveyor (inspection) + engineering partner (calculation).

Not a Marine Warranty Survey; within an MWS the lashing may be separately reviewed by the MWS surveyor.

Direct Video Survey
Purpose

Uses video as an evidentiary medium for an inspection (damage documentation, pre-purchase overview, remote evidence).

When needed

When physical attendance is limited or extra evidentiary support is required.

What is checked

Whatever the underlying survey requires, captured on video.

Report

Video file(s) + a written report referencing timestamps and findings.

Site/vessel visit

Can be physical (filming on board) or remote (live call).

Engineering calculation

No.

Who is responsible

The ALDEMARINE surveyor personally, based on reviewed footage.

This describes the evidence medium, not a class category. An official “Remote Survey” is a classification-society procedure performed only by the class's own surveyor — ALDEMARINE does not substitute for it.

Partner · subject to confirmation Vessel Condition Survey
Purpose

Independent assessment of a vessel's general physical and operational condition at a point in time.

When needed

At the request of an owner, insurer, financier or business partner.

What is checked

Hull, structure, machinery, safety equipment, documentation, housekeeping.

Report

Condition survey report with photos, findings and recommendations.

Site/vessel visit

Yes.

Engineering calculation

No.

Who is responsible

The appointed vessel-surveyor partner, personally.

Carries no class or regulatory standing and does not substitute a class survey. ALDEMARINE coordinates access to a partner; published as a direct service only once the specific surveyor's qualification is confirmed for the project.

Partner · subject to confirmation Bunker Survey
Purpose

Verifies the quantity (and sometimes quality) of fuel delivered to or remaining on board.

When needed

At bunkering or a change in the vessel's operating status.

What is checked

Tank soundings before/after transfer, density/temperature correction, sampling.

Report

Bunker survey report with reconciled quantity figures.

Site/vessel visit

Yes, presence during transfer.

Engineering calculation

No.

Who is responsible

The appointed vessel-surveyor partner + Chief Engineer/Master (co-signed).

A custody-transfer quantity check — not a cargo survey and not merged with on/off-hire figures.

Partner · subject to confirmation Draft Survey
Purpose

Determines total cargo weight loaded/discharged via a displacement calculation.

When needed

Before and after loading or discharge, when cargo quantity is confirmed by draft.

What is checked

Draft mark readings, water density, deductions (ballast/fresh water/constants).

Report

Draft survey report certifying cargo quantity.

Site/vessel visit

Yes.

Engineering calculation

Yes — a displacement calculation.

Who is responsible

The appointed vessel-surveyor partner, personally.

This is a weight-determination method only — not a cargo-condition or damage survey.

Partner · subject to confirmation On-Hire / Off-Hire Survey
Purpose

Records vessel condition and bunker quantity at the start (on-hire) and end (off-hire) of a charter.

When needed

At vessel delivery into charter and at redelivery to the owner.

What is checked

Holds/tanks, deck/cargo gear, structural condition, bunker soundings, certificate list.

Report

On-hire/off-hire report with signed bunker figures.

Site/vessel visit

Yes, at the delivery/redelivery port.

Engineering calculation

No.

Who is responsible

The appointed vessel-surveyor partner + Chief Engineer/Master (co-signed).

A commercial handover record — not a class, flag, damage or insurance survey.

Marine Warranty Survey (MWS)

ALDEMARINE provides Marine Warranty Survey support within the confirmed scope of the appointed surveyor. The applicable project scope, approval pathway and insurer requirements are confirmed before engagement.

Purpose

Independent review of non-routine marine/offshore operations to confirm the risk is acceptable to the insurer, as a policy condition.

When needed

When the insurer/underwriter requires MWS as a policy condition for a non-routine marine operation.

What is included

Attendance, inspection, review of documentation and procedures, and reporting — within an appointment by the client or insurer for a specific project.

Who signs

The appointed ALDEMARINE surveyor personally, within the confirmed competence and agreed scope of that appointment.

Class involvement

None.

Deliverable

MWS report / attendance note.

Important: Certificate of Approval is not issued by ALDEMARINE. Any formal Certificate of Approval, class approval or insurer acceptance remains subject to the requirements and decision of the relevant approving authority, insurer or appointed organisation. Service delivered by the appointed qualified surveyor through ALDEMARINE, subject to project scope, appointment and client requirements.
