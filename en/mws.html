Marine Warranty Survey (MWS)

ALDEMARINE coordinates Marine Warranty Survey and marine surveys with the appointed surveyor, who carries them out within the surveyor's confirmed competence and scope. Each engagement is subject to project requirements, client appointment and applicable insurer, class or project-owner conditions.

Important: Certificate of Approval is not issued by ALDEMARINE. Any formal class approval or insurer acceptance remains subject to the decision of the relevant approving authority.
